Privacy Policy — Serpent Wild: Apex

Effective Date: November 2026

Apex Wild Studios respects your privacy. All game progress (scores, skins, coins, diamonds, and settings) is stored locally on your device.

In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment information.

Notifications

Daily game reminders are scheduled locally on your device and can be disabled anytime in the in-game Settings.

Data Deletion

You can delete all local game data anytime from Player Dossier → Reset Data or by uninstalling the app.
